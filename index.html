05 / before you jump in

Questions.
Fair.

Do I need to be Indian?

/shor is Indian at its core, but anyone who likes the energy is welcome.

Do I need to be a gamer?

No. Gaming is one part of the server. A lot of people are here for the chat and voice calls.

Is it actually active?

That's the point. There is usually somebody around, especially once the sun goes down.

What are the rules?

Respect people. Keep the bakchodi fun, not cruel. No spam. Full rules live inside the server.

Can I just lurk?

Absolutely. You don't owe anyone an introduction. Lurk until you naturally find a conversation.
